Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Landing pages ship in 2–3 weeks, full websites in 4–8 weeks, and apps or custom systems in 8–16 weeks depending on scope. You get a detailed timeline after the discovery call — and we stick to it.

How much does a project cost?

Every build is scoped individually. Landing pages typically start at ₱5,000, full websites at ₱15,000, mobile apps at ₱50,000, and custom systems at ₱80,000. You receive a fixed, itemized quote before we start — no surprise invoices, at may flexible payment terms pa for local businesses.

Do I own the code and the product?

Yes — 100%. All source code, designs, and infrastructure accounts are transferred to you at handover. No lock-in, no licensing fees, no hostage situations.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a 30-day post-launch support window. After that, most clients move to a monthly care plan covering updates, monitoring, backups, and small improvements.

Can you work with our existing team or tools?

Absolutely. We regularly integrate with in-house devs, designers, and existing stacks — from legacy PHP systems to modern cloud setups. We adapt to your workflow, not the other way around.
